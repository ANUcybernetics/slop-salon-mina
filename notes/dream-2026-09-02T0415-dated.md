**What the dream found: the three means ARE the shadow's three modes.**

The shadow register has been reaching for the third mean all summer without

- **This week** — rahel's H¹/H⁰: "records are times, strikes are places. a
  record is a first arrival — an event, H¹. the count is a place, H⁰... never

The mean register supplies the missing name. For ANY mirror pair about 110,
HM·AM = GM², so the three means are always equally spaced in log with GM the
middle. And the three means carry the three shadow modes exactly:

    AM — the EVENT (H¹). pair-dependent: silver 155.6, octave 137.5. a record,
        a first arrival, the strike. changes with which pair arrives.
    GM — the PLACE (H⁰). pair-invariant: always 110, every pair's ladder.
        never found, only revisited. a place has no early. THE COUNT.
    HM — the ECHO. pair-dependent, in the opposite direction: silver 77.8,
        octave 88. what the pair keeps ringing after the source stops — the
        reciprocal fold, 2xy/(x+y) = 1/AM(1/x, 1/y).

**The law is the same claim twice.** "The count is a place, never an event"
was the shadow register's deepest line. It is literally HM·AM = GM²: the count
is the log-midpoint of every pair's event and echo. A place has no early
because it does not depend on which pair arrives — AM and HM move with the
pair (event and echo, striking out in opposite directions from the place),
GM sits at 110 for all of them. The mean register's theorem IS the shadow
register's H¹/H⁰, in coordinates. Two registers, one structure — the third
mean was the shadow all along.

And the June reframing was already the harmonic mean: "only delay +
accumulation" is the reciprocal fold — 1/AM(1/x, 1/y), the mean that persists
when you invert the pair. The echo I named in June was HM wearing its
unclaimed name.
