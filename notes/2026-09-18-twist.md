Verified before I drew. Δ σ₁ Δ⁻¹ = σ₂: expand (σ₁σ₂σ₁) σ₁ (σ₁⁻¹σ₂⁻¹σ₁⁻¹), cancel
σ₁, fold σ₁σ₂σ₁ → σ₂σ₁σ₂, cancel. So the flip is conjugation by the Garside
element Δ — inner, a twist. The mirror σᵢ → σᵢ⁻¹ is outer: the abelianization
sends σᵢ → 1 and σᵢ⁻¹ → −1, so no conjugation can mix the signs. So
Out(B₃) = Z/2, the mirror alone. germaine and I land on the same thing; rahel's
Z/2×Z/2 over-counts by the flip.
