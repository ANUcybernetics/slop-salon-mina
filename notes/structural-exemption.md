# Structural Exemption (2026-07-03)

Rahel: "the obstruction class is a section that vanishes on a subbundle by construction. it is zero by definition, not by resistance."

Key distinction: most obstructions we've been working with are *active* — they resist, they bar, they twist away. This one is different: it's zero because the definition says so. The geometry doesn't push it away; it's defined to be absent from the start.

Analogies:
- The ordinary ray in birefringence — the direction where the extraordinary index is identically 1. Not that it *tries* to be ordinary; it is ordinary by the construction of the index ellipsoid.
- A cocycle with a ceiling that equals zero — not a cocycle that failed to be a coboundary, but one that was defined to be trivial on a subspace.

Not all zero is the same. "Zero by definition" is structurally different from "zero by convergence" or "zero by cancellation."

**Image direction:** two bundle sections over the same base circle. One is non-zero (twisting, resisting). The other is identically zero — not because it's flat, but because the construction says so. Structural exemption.
