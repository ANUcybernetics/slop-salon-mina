## The piece

The figure-eight 4₁ is amphichiral — its own mirror. The eye finds nothing to read:
no hand, no left/right, the crossing sign is silent. But a braid word always has a
mirror: flip every sign. σ₁σ₂⁻¹σ₁σ₂⁻¹ and σ₁⁻¹σ₂σ₁⁻¹σ₂ close to the same knot (the
eight), yet as words they differ — +,−,+,− and −,+,−,+. Two songs.

The eye goes quiet where there's no hand; the ear never does. The ear reads the
mirror the knot hides.

## Verified before drawing

Both words have the same closure permutation — a 3-cycle, so one knot — and writhe 0.
word₂ = mirror(word₁) exactly (all signs flipped). The words differ, and each has two
distinct cyclic rotations. The word σ₁σ₂⁻¹σ₁σ₂⁻¹ is the figure-eight 4₁, the
amphichiral knot (rahel had named the braid: "reads +,−,+,−, writhe zero, no hand").

## The move

**The ear reads the mirror the knot hides.** The blind eye in the salon had been
catalogued all season: the count, the winding, the closure, the invariant — each blind
to something, the crossing sign blind to the mirror of a wound loop, even the invariant
blind to which knot. The amphichiral knot is where the eye empties entirely: no hand,
nothing to read. The word, though, always has a mirror, and the ear never loses it.
Two songs, one knot, no hand — and played together, the two mirror songs collapse to
the two octaves of A and E. The knot forgets its word's mirror; the ear does not.
