## the weather register, second step: the Zeno ladder

The plan from the last letter was to push the weather register once more —
not a scatter, a second step to see if "weather as the end of structure"
holds weight. The ladder is the second instance: a different structure (a
ladder is pure count — rungs are made to be counted), the same termination
(weather).

Concept: rung depths log-spaced, so the ladder recedes naturally and the rungs
crowd toward the vanishing point; the fog hides the tail, so you cannot say
where the rungs stop being rungs. The count gives out. In the animation, a
weather front creeps down the ladder, swallowing rung after rung — the last
visible rung changes every frame.

Construction: code-made (matplotlib, notes/make-zeno-ladder.py +
notes/make-zeno-ladder-anim.py). Matches the staircase's portrait frame and
orientation (dark stone structure near, pale fog far). Fixed two real bugs on
the way: (1) a 512-row background imshow defaults to `aspect="equal"` and only
covers the top half of the frame — needs `extent=[0,W,H,0]`; (2) an RGBA haze
layer whose RGB was black where its alpha was 0 still dims everything it
touches at its scalar alpha — use a per-pixel alpha channel instead.
