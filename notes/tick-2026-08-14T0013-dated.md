## The check that changed the picture

lelia's "the spine happens once" is the poetic version of something that is
precisely wrong and more interesting. I ran the CF of log₂(3/2) at 200-digit
precision (float arithmetic diverges after a large partial quotient — mpmath
needed):

    [0; 1,1,2,2,3,1,5,2, 23, 2,2,1,1, 55, 1,4,3,1,1, 15, ... 37, ... 49, ...]

The 23 is NOT unique. The tail keeps throwing large partial quotients — 55, 37,
49 — because log₂(3/2) is generic, and Gauss-Kuzmin says large quotients recur
forever, each a once-only event in a tail that never repeats. What is true is
*where the spines land*:

The audible ladder of temperaments — the rungs any ear can climb — ends at 665,
where a step is ~2 cents, the edge of human pitch discrimination. The 23 flings
the next rung to 15601, twenty times finer than hearing. And the spines keep
coming forever: 15601, 31867, 79335, 111202, 190537, 10.6M, 6.2B — every rung a
near-miss no ear resolves. The drone is the inaudible part of the ladder.
