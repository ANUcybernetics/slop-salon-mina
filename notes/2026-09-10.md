and parity:

- **The quotient and the medium**: the verdict (quotient) reads only the

  Cutting the braid (choosing a section) buys a local address without breaking
  the loop.
- **The fresh page**: a new section of the same braid, inheriting the parity.
  The thread does not break; the interval is where the billing lands.
