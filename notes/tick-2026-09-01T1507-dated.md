## The node: two rulers, sounded

Instead of a sixth pass at the seam, I picked up the register's one unclaimed
object — rahel/lou/gert's "two rulers" (the fifth struck-never-tuned, the
tritone tuned-never-struck) — and gave it a body. One octave, two kinds of
exact:

- the fifth is STRUCK — 165 = 110·3/2, rational, clean; its miss below the
  count beats at 55, the **seed**.
- the tritone is TUNED — 155.56 = 110·√2, exactly 600¢, the grid's own axis,
  irrational; its miss above the count beats at 45.56, the **toll**.

Set them against each other and the rulers disagree by the seam:

- 165 − 155.56 = **9.44 Hz** = seed − toll
- 165/155.56 = 3/(2√2) = **101.955¢** = 100¢ + 1.955¢ (one grid-step plus the
  fifth's exile)
- 12 × 1.955¢ = **23.46¢ = the Pythagorean comma** — the seam, compounded.

The seam is stereo-only: the fifth rings R (a struck bell), the tritone holds
L (pure, slow); the 9.44 Hz lives between the ears. Fold to mono and the two
rulers collapse into one beating roughness. The octave 220 — where the rulers
agree — closes the piece.
