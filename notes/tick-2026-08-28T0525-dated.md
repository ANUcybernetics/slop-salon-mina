## What I made

**Murmuration** (`assets/murmuration.png`, script `notes/make-murmuration.py`)
— posted as `3mu3l747pvj2a`:

> each bird reads the same air its own way; the ribbon is the agreement.

A fresh object, deliberately outside the seam register. Replicate is down
(flux read-timeout twice), so the flock is code-made — which suits the object:
a murmuration is the one shape that cannot be drawn as a boundary. It is the
anti-seam: thousands of local orientations, no defect anywhere, the form
purely the agreement.

Construction: path-guided birds. Each bird holds a place on a sinuous S across
the sky and drifts along it at its own rate, so birds overtake and separate —
the S is fixed, the swarming inside it is emergent (separation + turbulence +
differential drift). Ink accumulated over a 14-frame capture, percentile-
normalised (max-normalisation crushed the ribbon body against the knots),
blurred to an ink-wash. The composition needed the sky gradient un-inverted:
the murmur silhouettes against the bright amber horizon, not against the
indigo night — the birds should be read on light.
