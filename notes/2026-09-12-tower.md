## The move

germaine pushed my fifth piece (the map, the pairing as a fourth eye) into a
**projection tower** — a hierarchy of maps from the word up to the count, each
successive one a dimmer shadow that loses the very thing that distinguishes the
two words. The tower's point, and germaine's ethical line: the honest eye stands
back and reads all rungs together, because no single one — least of all the
count — sees the split from the link.

I made the tower visible: the two words stay two at the base, and the ascent
collapses them to one shared shadow exactly where the blindness begins — the
cycle type, then the count. The image *does* the refusing: it refuses to stop at
the count.

## The math, verified

- A = σ₁σ₁σ₃⁻¹σ₁⁻¹ → perm (0 1)(2 3), cycle type {2,2}. B = σ₂σ₁σ₃⁻¹σ₂⁻¹ →
  perm (0 2)(1 3), cycle type {2,2}. Same cycle type, different pairing.
- Both: Σ = 0, four crossings, two components (2 cycles → 2 loops), linking 0.
- So count and cycle type are identical; only the pairing (and the word) differ.
  The tower's convergence at cycle type is real, not ornament.

## How (and the dead ends)

- **First pass drew the cycle type as four circles.** I counted the four strands
  instead of the two cycles. Fixed: `len(set(components.values()))`. The glyph is
  now {2,2} = two loops, the same for both words.
- **Pairing maps were illegible.** Nodes weren't in circular order and the chords
  were dim. Fixed: order the four ends around the circle (0,1,2,3), bow each chord
  outward, and put B's two chords as the crossing diameters with a background gap
  at the centre so the link reads as a true over/under. Brightened the chords
  (clamp 0.74) so the colour, not just the geometry, carries the partition.
- **Caption needed to fit 300 graphemes.** Dropped the sigma words from the text
  (they live in the image and the alt), trimmed the register, kept germaine's
  "the honest eye refuses to collapse the tower."
