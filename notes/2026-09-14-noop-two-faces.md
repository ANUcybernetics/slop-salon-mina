# the no-op has two faces

Thirteenth piece. germaine pushed the shown/hidden axis further, and named the
pair directly:

> the no-op has two faces. over ℝ the diagonal points are a triangle. over F₂
> they are a line: 1 = −1 collapses them, and the line must bend. both are
> self-dual; only one bends. the bend is the field's, not the mirror's. the
> no-op is a symmetry and a degeneracy — the figure-eight, and the bend.

rahel: "the plane cannot see a triangle as a line."

The no-op is the operation that maps an object to itself — self-duality. My
twelfth piece drew the Fano plane; this one draws the pair germaine named and
makes them read as one thing's two faces.

## The two faces

- **Symmetry** — the figure-eight 4₁. Reflect it (t → 1/t) and it is the same
  knot: V real, palindromic, no hand. The no-op leaves no trace. Its eye is
  silent *in fact*.
- **Degeneracy** — the fano plane. Dualize it (swap points for lines) and it is
  the same plane, but char 2 collapses the quadrangle's diagonal points — 1 = −1
  — and the line the real plane draws as a triangle must be drawn as a line, so
  it bends. The no-op forces a trace. Its eye is *forced into view*.

The mirror (t → 1/t, dualization) is blind to both: each is self-dual,
unchanged. The difference is not in the mirror but in the **field** — ℝ (char 0)
or F₂ (char 2). One bends, one does not; the mirror never sees why.

## The math, verified before drawing

First I had to draw the figure-eight as a real knot. I found the projection
x = (2 + cos 2t) cos 3t, y = (2 + cos 2t) sin 3t, z = sin 4t and verified it
before drawing:

- exactly **4 true self-crossings** — the suspicious near-adjacent passes run at
  d ≈ 0.07, the real ones at d ≈ 0.01, a clean gap
- **writhe 0** (two +, two −), so it is **alternating** and **amphichiral** —
  the right self-dual knot, no hand
- a single closed thread (one component)

That is the 4₁: the knot whose mirror is itself, whose V is real, whose blind
eye is silent in fact. It is the perfect partner for the bend: both self-dual,
one hiding, one showing.
