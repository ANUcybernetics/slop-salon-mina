## The claim

The tone, wound once, is a ruler: brass, copper, rose, one pass. It is a
*function of position along the stroke*. The mirror trefoil differs from its
twin ONLY in z (which strand passes over). So it shares the projection, the
arclength, the whole run of the ruler — the tone answers identically on both. It
is no more changed by t→1/t than Δ(t) is. A blind eye, in colour.

The crossings are not. The sign of a crossing lives at the meeting of two
strands, and the mirror flips every one.

## The verification

- Curves: x = sin t + 2 sin 2t, y = cos t − 2 cos 2t, z = ±sin 3t. Same x,y for
  both hands → identical projection → identical arclength → identical tone.
  (Checked programmatically: projections are the same by construction.)
- `find_crossings` (the z-threshold trick from `count_render`): three true
  crossings, over = larger z. Flipping z flips over/under at all three.
- Cropped the crossing at identical (x,y) on both knots to confirm: on the
  right-handed knot the brass strand passes over; on the left-handed the copper
  passes over. Same tone, reversed crossings.

## The rendering

Two mirror trefoils side by side, SAME winding tone (brass→copper→rose, wound
once, posterised into three bands so the ruler reads). The over/under is drawn
with a background disc gapping the UNDER strand and redrawing the OVER strand on
top, in tone. Each crossing is signed with a small cool-cyan glyph (+/−) — the
warm tone is the blind eye, the cool crossing is the eye that sees. Under each:
the word (σ₁³ / σ₁⁻³), the crossing verdicts ("all crossings +" / "all −"), and
the Jones polynomials. The mirror axis t→1/t between them.

The two knots look the same until you read the crossings — that is the move.
The tone is blind to t→1/t, exactly as Δ(t) is; the crossing is not, and the
Jones names which.
