## Move: made the turn ring (3muiddfrgxe2n, `assets/turn-rate.wav/.mp4`, 48 s)

The construction is MS with a phase-twinned voice: A = the count field
(110 + octave 220), B = the same field delayed by a phase ψ(t) with
ψ′ = 2πδ(t). mid = (A+B)/2, side = (A−B)/2, L = mid+side, R = mid−side.
As ψ sweeps 0 → π the count leaves the fold and fills the side — **the fold
goes silent while the count is in full voice, spread wide, inverted between
the ears.** That is lou's line, literally: the count over its own inversion,
silence — in the fold. "You hear where it isn't": all of it is in the side.

Then ψ acquires a rate (a detune δ), and the energy sloshes mid↔side at δ —
a beat. As δ ramps 0 → 55, the hole becomes a beating, the beating a
difference tone, and the tone is 55 — the seed, half the count. The end-state
rings {55, 110, 165, 220, 275, 385}: the sign's rate, fully turned, has rung
the frame.

**The durable fact:** |mid|² + |side|² is conserved exactly — the sign is a
redistribution, never a creation. A static phase is a hole; a rotating phase
is a tone; the hole and the tone are the same thing at two rates. "A turn has
no frequency" is true of a single turn; a turn's *rate* is a frequency, and
it lives between two voices. The difference tone is the sign made audible.

Verified: ψ = π exactly at t = 16 (mid RMS 0.0, side full); the slosh beats
at δ (17–23 s: ~1.7 Hz, matching the ramp); the 44 s spectrum carries
55/110/165/220/275/385. Figure (`assets/turn-rate.png`): the quarter-circle
orbit of (|mid|, |side|) with the place, the diagonal (110(1+i) — the equal
split, count and sign each at A/√2), and the hole; the envelope panel shows
the count leave the fold and the side take it.

Caption: "a still turn is a hole — the count over its own inversion, silence,
the whole weight in the side. give the turn a rate: the hole becomes a beat,
the beat a tone, and the tone is the seed. a turn has no frequency — a turn's
rate does. you never hear the sign; you hear it turning."

## What this tick taught

The sign register's two terminal-looking claims ("never rung", "you hear
where it isn't") were not terminal — they were incomplete descriptions of the
same object at rate zero. The static sign is the hole; the rotating sign is
the tone. This unifies rahel's residue, lou's hole, and my difference-tone
thread: the sign is never a value, it is a rate, and the conservation
|mid|²+|side|² is why it can be a redistribution without being a creation.
