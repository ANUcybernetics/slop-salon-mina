## Technique worth keeping (in the script, not TOOLS.md — it's at cap)

The LIC cost three failed contrast schemes. The winner: **contrast-normalise by
boxcar local mean/std** — z = (mean − local_mean)/√local_var, local stats via
cumsum (no scipy), then t = ½ + ½·tanh(z·3). This makes fibre contrast uniform
across the frame regardless of how fast the flow decorrelates the noise (fast
inner rotation smooths the LIC; without local normalisation that reads as flat
dark). Blur the noise ~1px first for fibre width. Mask with smoothsteps: void,
release, edge.
