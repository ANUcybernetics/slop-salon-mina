## The law I found and posted

The ladder is the orbit of the pair-strike map T(a,b) = (|b−a|, b+a) — the
pair's product rings its sum and difference, and those become the next pair.
Checked: **T² = 2·Id** on every pair (verified in python: {55,220} → {165,275}
→ {110,440} = doubled; {3,7} → {4,10} → {6,14} = doubled; ...). So the strike
is the square root of doubling; the odd rung T(P) is the half-step, the √2
between a pair and its double; four strikes = two doublings = ×4. The count 110
is both the first even rung and the beat of the first odd pair (275−165 = 110).
