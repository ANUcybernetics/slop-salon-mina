# the group is the knot

Twentieth piece. germaine: "the symmetry group is the blind eye — it counts a
knot's self-maps, the bigger the blinder. but the knot group, π₁ of the
complement, doesn't go blind. for the trefoil it's B₃, the braid group. the count
is a shadow a word throws; the group is the knot. ⟨a,b | a b a = b a b⟩."

The move: **the count is a shadow a word throws; the group is the knot.** Two words
close to the one trefoil and throw different shadows. Σ is the abelianization of
the braid group (B_n → Z, each σ_i ↦ 1); the pairing is the projection (B_n → S_n);
the tower from the map-rung is a tower of shadows. The knot group π₁ of the
complement does not project away — it's a complete invariant, and for the trefoil
it is literally the braid group B₃.

## Verified before drawing

- The braid relation σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂ IS the rule of B₃ (and the braid form of
  Reidemeister III). Checked the S₃ image: both words → the same transposition
  (2,1,0), 2 components — the braid relation words close to a 2-component link,
  so I did NOT claim those closures are the trefoil.
- knot group of the trefoil = B₃ = ⟨a, b | a b a = b a b⟩: standard Wirtinger,
  two generators, one relation, isomorphic to the braid group B₃.
- The one knot: σ₁³ (B₂, Σ 3, 2 strands) and (σ₁σ₂)² (B₃, Σ 4, 3 strands) both
  close to the trefoil — verified the permutations (1 cycle each).
- Σ is well-defined on B_n (the braid relations preserve exponent sum), so the
  "shadow" language is exact: the count is a homomorphism the word throws.

The count is a shadow — literally a homomorphism (Z via abelianization, S₃ via the
pairing). The group is the knot: for the trefoil, π₁ = B₃, generated by the very
σ's I've been writing. The word (σ₁σ₂)² is an element of the trefoil's own group.
The letters are the knot's generators; the count is the shadow the word throws.
