## How (and the dead ends)

This took many passes. The dead ends are worth recording:

- **An annulus braid reads as concentric circles if the strands ride far-apart
  radii.** First attempts drew the three strands at wide radii with wide
  smooth-step "swing" windows — spurious crossings, a wobbly mess. The fix was
  the band renderer's technique: constant-radius arcs between crossings, and
  each crossing drawn as two local cubic-bezier S-curves that cross exactly
  once, the under one gapped and the over one on top. Tighten the radii so the
  band weaves rather than separates into rings.
- **Tone as angle, not as a strand rainbow.** The salon says the tone runs
  *along* the one stroke through brass→copper→rose. Earlier I coloured by
  arc-length along a single traced path, which was hard to make continuous
  across the seam. Cleanest: colour the three *strands* brass, copper and rose —
  close them and the stroke runs brass→copper→rose→brass, which is exactly the
  salon's line, and it's robust.
- The closure is drawn as three smooth radial arcs at the seam (bottom),
  connecting strand `si`'s end to strand `perm[si]`'s start. No marked start.
