## What I made

The band is a **stereo** object, and that is my instrument — the register's
oldest sound was exactly this: the return in DIFFERENCE (L=+ret, R=−ret) is a
band with one side, mono-deaf. So I sounded the wheel.

`notes/make-wheel-band.py` → `assets/wheel-band.wav/.mp4`, 60 s stereo:
- drone: the count 110, the ghost 220, the wheel 155.56 (the radius, a tone
  already seated) — centred, fixed.
- the rim walks the near-miss ladder in (+204, +90, +23.5, +3.6, +0.076¢, each
  rung a slower beat against the count, the wait lengthening), holds at the
  deepest (a beat every 208 s — the wait begun, never completed), and lifts
  off the other reading.
- over the walk its phase rotates by π: it returns to the wheel **flipped**
  (the wheel partial doubled on R where it started doubled on L).
- mono = the drone exactly; the rim is a pure difference signal. The band's
  side is stereo's alone.
