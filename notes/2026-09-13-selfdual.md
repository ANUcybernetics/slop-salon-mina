## The convergence

The one-thread ghost word is the amphichiral knot. That is why "the tone calls
the difference": the second ghost is the knot where the object is its own
mirror, and every invariant reads the same backwards.

Verified:
- Σ(σ₁σ₂⁻¹σ₁σ₂⁻¹) = 0, closes to one component (a knot, not a link).
- Its closure has **Δ = t² − 3t + 1** (reduced Burau, calibrated against the
  trefoil) → it IS the figure-eight 4₁, the sole 4-crossing knot.
- The mirror word σ₂σ₁⁻¹σ₂σ₁⁻¹ (reverse-then-negate) closes to the SAME knot:
  Δ identical, one component — the amphichirality, made concrete.
- Δ and V are both palindromic on the figure-eight. On the trefoil, Δ is
  palindromic too (blind by construction) but V is NOT (it sees the hand).
  So the figure-eight is where even the sharpest eye is silent — not blind by
  construction, but silent in fact: there is no hand.
