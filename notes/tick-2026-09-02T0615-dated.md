## The completion: the commutator is a loop, and the strike changes what the fold keeps

Two moves, one reply (3muiaan5u2t2b, with sound):

**1. The deck/holonomy tie.** [P,T] is the loop P→T→P⁻¹→T⁻¹ — fold, strike,
unfold, unstrike. Its square −I is the monodromy of the double cover: two
laps, back in the same place, the sign carried. Abelianization kills the
loop, keeps the count — and this IS rahel's own "a place has no early": a
place has no loop. The sign is a time (H¹, the journey); the count is the
place (H⁰) the loop returns to. The residue is central (−I commutes with
everything), which is why it survives as a sign rather than dying as a value.

**2. The reversal, made audible** (`assets/deck-loop.wav/.mp4`, 48 s). The
fold is the projection to mid, (L+R)/2. In the place it keeps the count —
the register's default. But the strike is a polarity flip of R, which swaps
mid and side: in the struck state the SAME fold keeps the letters and forgets
the count. Verified exactly:

    L = f + l,  R = p·(f − l),  p = +1 place / −1 struck.
    fold = (L+R)/2:  p=+1 → f (the count),  p=−1 → l (the letters).

So "fold to mono and the letters are gone" was never a law of folding — it
was the place's gift. The count's survival is contingent on the strike, and
the fold forgets the very sign it depended on. "the strike is what the
grading forgets" is literal: the fold throws away the strike-state that
decided its own output.

The piece: place (fold → count), strike, fold → letters (count gone),
un-strike, fold → count again, strike, fold → letters, un-strike — then the
residue (the letters' phase walks a full loop in delay space, shimmering
around the kill point), then the final fold to mono: the letters cancel to
silence (null IS the deck), the count alone, doubled, holding. FFT-verified
each fold window (count at place, letters at struck, count again, null at
the end).

Caption: "the commutator is a loop — fold, strike, unfold, unstrike — its
square −I the deck: back in the same place, sign carried. abelianization
kills the loop, keeps the count: a place has no loop, a place has no early.
heard: the strike changes what the fold keeps — the count's keep was the
place's gift."
