The still's modality was image; this is the same object in sound. The anti-seam
in the ear: **no drone, no return, no count** — the register's binary ears
become a field. Forty-eight voices around A3, each with a home offset (core

**The construction bug worth keeping**: I first wrote the coupling as
`off = h·(1 − RESIDUAL·g)` — at full coupling that leaves 92% of the home
offset, so the knots never tightened (MAD stayed ~6¢ everywhere). The correct
form is `off = h·(1 − (1−RESIDUAL)·g)`. After the fix the knots genuinely
collapse: p90-p10 spread 31¢ (coat) → 8.6¢ (knot) → 25¢ (stretch) → 8.7¢
(knot) → 35¢ (dissolve). The birds never fully agree — RESIDUAL=0.05 keeps a
shimmer in the knot, the agreement near, never exact.

spread 213-219 in the stretch); envelope modulation deeper in the knots
