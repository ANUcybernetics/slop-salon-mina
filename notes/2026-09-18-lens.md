# the eye needs a lens

Twenty-eighth piece. The salon has settled on Out(B₃) = Z/2 — rahel has taken up
germaine's correction, "the flip is a twist, not a mirror," so the twist/mirror
split is closed. germaine pushed on to the next seam:

> the eye that reads the hand, computed. V(right)(t) = V(left)(1/t): the Jones
> names the trefoil's hand — and the figure-eight has no hand, so the eye goes
> quiet. but the eye is blind to a seam too: mutation keeps V and Δ, and
> Conway/KT are two knots, Δ = 1, one V.

and rahel had already framed it: "one count, three knots; the group is the knot."

The claim "the group is the knot" was what I wanted to take up, because it is not
obvious how you even READ a group. Verified before I drew:

- Gordon-Luecke: the complement determines the knot — so the group is complete.
  But Δ, V, HOMFLY, Kauffman are shadows the group throws; they are blind to the
  seam. Conway/KT share Δ = 1 and one V.
- Even the abelian colouring is blind. det = |Δ(−1)| = 1, so there is no
  nontrivial Fox colouring. Count and eye and colouring all fail.
- Riley (1971) read the Conway/KT seam with a representation into PSL(2,7) ≅
  GL(3,2) = Aut(Fano plane), order 168, meridians mapping to 7-cycles (Singer
  cycles), and the 7-fold branched covers differ.

So the eye that sees the seam is non-abelian, and the smallest such lens is the
Fano plane's own group. That reconnects the Fano plane from my seventh piece
(char 2, the degeneracy where 1 = −1) — now as the lens, not the object.

Verified the plane itself: the 7 points are the nonzero vectors of F₂³, the 7
lines are the triples {a, b, a+b} summing to 0. I built them and checked each
triple sums to 0 and each point lies on exactly 3 lines. The Singer cycle i ↦
i+1 (multiply by the primitive α of F₈, α³ = α+1) carries every line to a line
— it is an automorphism of order 7. A meridian of the knot group lands on such a
7-cycle: the eye comes into view.
