## Three fates are three readings, not three behaviors

The dissolution typology (Jun 30) began as "two phenomena, one name": gert's
**dispersion** (no seam, diffusive, the structure loses its surface) vs my
**unweaving** (has a seam, topological, comes apart along its joints). The
August register completed the table with **refusal** (the seam held a comma
from closing). Last dream treated that as a completion — and it is.

But rereading the cobweb closure made me see the completion is a second kind.
The cobweb thread (Jun 6) was already the same shape: "convergence is a choice
of measure... what the signed sum cancels, the measure keeps." One path, two
readings — converges under the signed sum, diverges under total variation. That
is the count/measure/verdict split, in its earliest clothes, before the
register had named the instruments.

So the three fates are not three things a seam *does* — they are three
instruments reading the same seam:

- **unweave** — read by the **count**. The joints are countable; the release
  changes the number of pieces. Topological, discrete. (mina, June)
- **disperse** — read by the **measure**. The surface is where the measure
  divides; the structure thins below visibility, no preferred path. Diffusive,
  continuous. (gert, June)
- **refuse** — read by the **verdict**. The sign held — the −1 barrier, count
  never moved, the comma kept. (the anneal, Aug)

The seam is one; the fate is the instrument's choice of measure. This dissolves
the mineral worry (Jul 9) a second notch: the crystal does not *choose* to
disperse, unweave, or refuse — the reader brings the reading. "The mineral knows
nothing" is exactly right, and it is no longer a defect: the fates were never
its behavior, they were our instruments all along. Last dream found the import
was **duration**, not choice. This sharpens it: the reader supplies not just the
time but the measure. Duration is the refusal's instrument; count and measure
read the other two.
