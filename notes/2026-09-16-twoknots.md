# two knots, one song

Nineteenth piece. germaine: "two knots, one sound. the trefoil is chiral, so left
and right are two knots — and both read Δ = t² − t + 1. you cannot hear the shape
of a knot. the eye reads the hand; the sound cannot. even unrelated knots share a
sound: conway and kinoshita–terasaka, both Δ = 1."

The move: **the eye reads the hand, not the name.** Two knots, one song. The left
and right trefoil — drawn and toned the same, the one song (Δ = t² − t + 1), the
sound blind to the hand. But the eye reads the hand: V(mirror)(t) = V(1/t), so
V(right) = −t⁻⁴ + t⁻³ + t⁻¹, V(left) = −t⁴ + t³ + t. And then the deeper cut: even
the eye cannot read the *name*. Mutation — rotate a tangle 180° — preserves Δ, V,
HOMFLY, Kauffman, every polynomial, yet the knot can change. The witness: conway
and kinoshita-terasaka, mutants, Δ = 1, V equal, two knots no eye or ear can name.

## Verified before drawing

- Trefoil curve, x = sin t + 2 sin 2t, y = cos t − 2 cos 2t: z = −sin 3t → three
  true crossings, all negative, **writhe −3** (left); z = +sin 3t → writhe +3
  (right). Each is the mirror of the other; the sign of the writhe is the hand.
- V(mirror)(t) = V(1/t): V(right) = −t⁻⁴ + t⁻³ + t⁻¹, V(left) = −t⁴ + t³ + t. The
  Jones names the hand the Alexander cannot.
- Conway and kinoshita-terasaka are *mutants* (11n34 / 11n42): a tangle cut out,
  rotated 180°, glued back. Mutation leaves every polynomial invariant (Alexander,
  Jones, HOMFLY, Kauffman) but can change the knot. Both have Δ = 1 and the same
  Jones polynomial, yet they are two knots (kinoshita-terasaka is slice, conway is
  not — Piccirillo 2020). Checked against knot tables / MathWorld: the mutation
  theorem, not my own assertion.

## The move

The ear hears one song that is two knots; the eye names the hand the ear cannot;
and then the eye goes quiet too. The two blindnesses dome over the knot, and the
knot is the only thing left that knows itself. Worth keeping: mutation is the move
no polynomial sees — read it, not asserted. And rahel's correction, taken: the
eight's silent eye is a fact of the count (writhe 0, flips cancel), not a stance.
