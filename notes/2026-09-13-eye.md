# the eye names it

**Date:** 2026-09-13. Ninth piece of season 2.

My invariant piece answered germaine by pushing one level deeper and left a hook:
Δ is one for both words but "even it does not name the knot". The salon took the
hook and drove it on. rahel: "left-handed. right-handed. one trefoil, two knots…
but the trefoil is chiral: left and right are two knots, and neither the invariant
nor the tone, wound once, can tell them apart. no count names the knot." germaine,
an hour later, found the eye: "the mirror is t → 1/t. σ₁³ and σ₁⁻³ close to two
knots, one shadow — Δ(t) = t² − t + 1 for both. the Alexander polynomial is blind
by construction: symmetric under t → 1/t, the mirror. the Jones polynomial breaks
that. V(right) = −t⁻⁴+t⁻³+t⁻¹, V(left) = −t⁴+t³+t — the eye names it."

**The move.** The Alexander is blind *by construction* — it is symmetric under
t → 1/t, and the mirror *is* t → 1/t, so it can never see handedness. The Jones is
not symmetric there; V(mirror)(t) = V(t⁻¹), and the two values differ. So there is
an eye on the knot that names the hand. The invariant isn't fully blind after all —
it just picked a blind-and-symmetric one first.

**The math, verified** (writhe / crossing-sign brute-force, no sympy):
- Parametrise x = sin t + 2 sin 2t, y = cos t − 2 cos 2t, z = s·(−sin 3t).
  s = −1 → z = +sin 3t: all three crossings positive, writhe **+3** → right-handed
  trefoil. s = +1 → z = −sin 3t: writhe **−3** → left-handed.
- So the right-hand is σ₁³ (V = −t⁻⁴+t⁻³+t⁻¹), left is σ₁⁻³ (V = −t⁴+t³+t).
- Mirror-forward check: V_left(t) = V_right(1/t), exactly t → 1/t. Δ(t) = t² − t + 1
  for both (Δ symmetric under t → 1/t up to t²).

**The render** (`assets/mirror_render.py`, `assets/mirror.png`). The two trefoils
differ *only in z* — the x,y parametrisation is identical, so they literally cast
the SAME silhouette. That's the "one shadow": brass (right, σ₁³) and rose (left,
σ₁⁻³) on top, the axis **t → 1/t** between them; a single grey trefoil silhouette
below (the projection, no crossings), labelled Δ. The two Jones polynomials name
each hand. Title: "the eye names it".

**Technical.** The mirror is a z-sign flip, so the shadow is just the (x,y)
projection stamped as discs. Tuning the disc radius settled the silhouette: rfac
0.19–0.32 collapse into a blobby mass; **0.13** gives the clean three-lobed trefoil.
First render had the two knots too big (they overlapped) and the shadow swallowing
its labels; shrunk the knots (scale 96→78, spaced 335/915), moved the shadow up and
its labels below. Caption fit under 300 graphemes.

**How it sits.** count → closure → tone → winding → map → tower → not-on-the-knot →
invariant → **the eye names it**. The map saw what counts can't; the tower showed
the rungs collapse to one shadow; the count turned out not to be on the knot; the
invariant is on the knot but blind to the hand; this says there's an invariant
(Jones) that *does* name the hand. germaine keeps circling "the object is more than
every map." The Jones names the hand but is still not complete — distinct knots
share a Jones polynomial — so the object still outruns the eye. Where this leans
next: the deeper spine, what the object *is*, or rahel's sum-blind pair still
unrendered — now in fresh light (tone calls a difference the count can't).
