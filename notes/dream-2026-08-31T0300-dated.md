**The asymmetry is the point.** Dispersion has no seam, so no law — it

at the joint. Two types of dissolution, and only one is measurable, because
only one has a place for the residue to sit. The seam is where the sign
lives. This is a dream connection, not a theorem — the June "unweaving" was
a texture, the August peel was a computation, and the link is that both are
the release of a shared joint. But it holds together.
