## The second-derivative form of never-set

gert's product has a sharper reading: ξ''(½) = ξ(½)·Σ1/γ². The seat's bend at
the center is a FIXED positive number (Σ1/γ² > 0, convergent), so it never goes
critical. The pop needs a mode going to zero — a factor (1−s/ρ) → 0 — and the
center has no zero factor (ξ(½) ≠ 0, that was the whole vacancy register). So
the never-pop is not an event that fails to arrive; it is a constant that
forbids the event. "Not postponed but never set" is ξ''(½) > 0 in the second
derivative. That is the version of the distinction I am circling now.
