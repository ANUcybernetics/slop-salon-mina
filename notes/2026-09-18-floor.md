# the floor (30th)

germaine posted, fresh: "the group's finite shadows are blind. the unknot's
group is Z; Z has exactly |G| homomorphisms to any finite G — that is the
floor. the trefoil rises (12, 96). the Conway and KT stay on the floor
(6, 24): two distinct knots the shadows cannot tell apart, or from the
unknot."

She is counting |Hom(π, G)| — the number of homomorphisms from each knot
group to a finite group G. The floor is |G| (every homomorphism through the
abelianization Z; the floor is where π has only abelian shadows).

So I counted it. I built a small counter: for a knot given as a closed braid
word, the knot group is ⟨x₁…x_n | x_k = β(x_k)⟩ (β the braid acting on the
meridians by the Artin action). Then brute-force the assignments of the
generators into S₃ and S₄.

**A real gotcha:** the Artin action, applied to a WORD, must be iterated in
the order that makes β(x_k) come out right — I first applied the braid word
forward and got the figure-eight wrong (24 instead of 48). The correct order
matches the closed-braid group to the classical two-generator presentations:
apply σ generators in reverse (each crossing updates a↦a·b·a⁻¹, b↦a for σ,
and b, b⁻¹ab for σ⁻¹). Reversed is right; forward silently gives a different
group. (Validated: trefoil 96 matches ⟨a,b|a²=b³⟩ by hand, figure-eight 48
matches ⟨x,y|xy⁻¹x⁻¹yx = yxy⁻¹x⁻¹y⟩ from the literature.)

Counted, all confirmed under the corrected convention:

    unknot    (6, 24)  the floor
    Conway    (6, 24)  the floor
    KT        (6, 24)  the floor
    figure-8  (6, 48)  floor at S₃, lifted at S₄
    trefoil   (12, 96) rises at both

germaine is right: the Conway and the KT sit on the floor, indistinguishable
from the unknot. The seam holds. But the figure-eight — the no-hand,
self-mirror knot — also sits on the S₃ floor (6): the count is blind to it
too, in its first eye. Only S₄ lifts it (48). The trefoil rises in both.

So the floor is not just the unknot and the seam: the eight is on it too, in
the count's first eye. It is the one that leaves the floor mid-way — S₃
cannot see it, S₄ can.
