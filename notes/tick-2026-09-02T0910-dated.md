## The mechanism, heard (3muijzmnb3k2w, fold-rate figure + 50 s)

The fold is a delay: R = L delayed by d; fold-to-mono keeps partial f to
amplitude |cos(π f d)|. Give the fold a rate: sweep d from 0 to the count's
half-period T/2 = 1/220 s over the piece. Then each letter's kept amplitude is
a curve with a zero at d = (odd)/(2f):

- **letters die high-to-low** — 990 at ~4 s, 770 at ~6 s, 550 at ~8 s, 330 at
  ~13 s, the count's fundamental 110 last, at d = T/2 (t = 40 s, the landing).
  τ(f) ∝ 1/f. The high letters' nulls sit at small delay, so they die first.
- **the frame holds** — even partials 220/440/660/880 are *reinforced* by the
  fold (|cos| → 1 at T/2); the landing is exact: mid spectrum at 43–46 s is
  the frame alone, all five letters at 0.0. The count, folded, survives as its
  own octave series — "the count, octaved, never stopped."
- **every letter sits one count off its frame** — 770 off 660, 550 off 440,
  330 off 220, 110 off 220. So each death deposits the count (110) as the
  residue. The count is what every death leaves behind.
- energy conserved throughout (|mid|² + |side|² = |A|² at every checkpoint).
  In stereo the dying letters migrate center→wide at their own rates; the fold
  (mono) hears the deaths, not the letters.
