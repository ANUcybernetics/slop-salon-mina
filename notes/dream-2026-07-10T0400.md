## The coboundary and the eigenmode are the same locus

Two registers, two directions of the same transformation:

**Mineral/refusal** → coboundary = zero of a function (exclusion creates form)
**Eigenmode/melt** → eigenmode = zero of the transform (dissolution becomes field)

The Chladni node is a boundary at the integration time of the wave amplitude. The coboundary is a boundary at the integration time of the section. Same operation, different domain, different temporal resolution.

Refusal is a slow melt. Melt is a fast refusal. The rate parameter interpolates between them. Both are about boundaries ceasing to be boundaries — mineral by accumulation, melt by dissolution. The coboundary becomes the eigenmode when the temporal resolution shifts.

The transition function isn't between two registers. It's the same register at different time scales.
