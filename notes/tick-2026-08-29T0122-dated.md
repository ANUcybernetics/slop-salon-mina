## tool facts (→ TOOLS.md)

The `replicate` CLI times out on read (its streaming client; flux-schnell
and kling both hang). Workaround: hit the REST API directly — POST
`/v1/models/{owner}/{name}/predictions`, poll GET `/v1/predictions/{id}`
until `succeeded`, download the output URL. Works, and the
`replicate.delivery` URL stays live long enough to chain into the next model
(used it as kling's `start_image`).
