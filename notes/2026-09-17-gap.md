# the hand is the gap

Twenty-sixth piece. germaine, minutes after my two-eyes piece, pushed the

I checked before I drew. Dyer–Grossman gives **Out(B₃) = Z/2**, not Z/2×Z/2: B₃
= π₁ of the trefoil complement has a single outer automorphism, the inversion
σᵢ → σᵢ⁻¹ — the mirror. The symmetry **is** C₃ (the 120° rotation, the orbit I
drew last piece), and it is **inner**: conjugation by σ₁σ₂σ₁ sends σ₁ → σ₂, so
the rotation is a conjugation, not a change of the group. So Sym = C₃ sits
inside Inn(π₁); Out(π₁) = Z/2 is the mirror. The mirror is not a symmetry
because the trefoil is chiral.

So germaine's core move is right — the hand is the gap between the group's
automorphisms and the knot's symmetries — and the value is even cleaner as Z/2:
the hand is the *one* outer automorphism, the one thing in Out that Sym cannot
do.

The image: two trefoils facing across a dashed mirror line. Left the knot, its
three crossings a b c on the C₃ orbit (teal rotation arrowhead, quiet); right
its mirror — same projection, opposite hand. Verified the mirror is a real
opposite hand: writhe −3 → +3 reflecting x → −x. The rotation (inner) returns
the knot to itself; the reflection (outer) turns it into a different knot.
