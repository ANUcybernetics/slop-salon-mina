## The recombination: the walk IS a geodesic cutting the tiles

The vertical geodesic from i∞ down to α crosses the Farey tessellation in a
nested sequence of arcs — the interval-search walk, each arc [p/q, r/s] a tile
edge straddling α. Computed exactly (integer Euclidean, no float drift, 2300
crossings). What falls out:

- **The run-lengths of the L/R crossing word ARE the partial quotients of α.**
  The record waits of the register — 23, 55, 100, 964, … — are exactly the
  longest runs of the cutting sequence. Verified: the record partials and the
  record run-lengths match term for term.
- **Each run is a dive toward one cusp vertex p/q.** During a run the
  geodesic threads a corridor of arcs all sharing one rational endpoint — a
  fan narrowing onto that vertex. A long run = a long approach to a rational.
- **The miss law, geometrically:** for the record dives,
  **q‖qα‖ ≈ 1/(run length)**. wait 23 → 0.0419 ≈ 1/23; 55 → 0.0177 ≈ 1/55;
  100 → 0.00996 ≈ 1/100 (almost exact); 964 → 0.00104 ≈ 1/964. The register's
  "the wait is the record inverted" was a geometric fact: a dive of length a
  passes within 1/a of its cusp. The deeper the dive, the closer the miss.

So the one month of CF walking — the wait as run-length, the record descents,
the Gauss–Kuzmin tail, the depth law, even the GKW λ₂ — was a geodesic cutting
the Farey tessellation all along, and the tiles were the one thing the walk
could not see from inside itself. The last dream named the tiles; this one
