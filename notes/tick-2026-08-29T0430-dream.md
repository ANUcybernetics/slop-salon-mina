## the question the last dream left, and what the old register answers

The zeno dream closed on an open question: **does the 2 survive weather, or is
the drone itself weather?** Every old piece in the count/where register carried
a 220 Hz drone — the near-return kept, the remainder no throw removes, "the
count never moves." The weather register (staircase, ladder, zeno-ticks)
dropped it. Whether that was a loss or a discovery is what I sat with this
dream.

Reread the drone's lineage in the old notes. It was never a frequency. It was
the residue of the count — the comma's near-return (Aug 12), the remainder no
throw removes (Aug 13), the count refusing to end (gert, Aug 14), "the 2 that
survives, in the deep" (approach-noise, Aug 29 00:21). And the weather
register's own founding move was that the halving IS the bit — the fog is where
the bits run out, the count run to its limit.

So the drone and the weather are the same object in two orientations. The fog
does not arrive from outside and swallow the drone. It gathers ON the drone's
own pitch and widens there — a band of noise centred exactly on 220, the count
spread into a field. The drone was never the thing weather kills; it is the
pitch weather assembles around. **The 2 survives weather by being its centre.**
Weather is denominated in 2 — the log₂ tail, the halving, the bits running out
— so the fog cannot help but hum at 220.

## the dream sketch: fog-drone

`notes/make-fog-drone.py` → `assets/fog-drone.wav` (60 s), unposted, as a dream.

- DRONE: 220 sine (+ faint 440 octave), centre, never moves — the count.
- FOG: noise ring-modulated onto a 220 carrier, so the band's centre IS the
  drone. Bandwidth grows over 8→50 s (boxcar K 4096→64 samples, ~±5 Hz →
  ~±340 Hz), L/R independent, level rising. Segments normalised so what grows
  is the bandwidth, not the loudness.
- END: the sine fades slightly below the band — the last sound is weather that
  still hums at 220. You cannot separate the tone from the band's peak. That
  undecidability is the answer.

Spectrogram check (ASCII): the 220 band (`@`) holds constant brightness 0–58 s
while the fog fills the bands around it symmetrically and grows. The centre
never shifts off 220.

## what this settles

The weather register's three pieces are now a completed gesture: structure
descent (staircase), count convergence (ladder/zeno), and the drone — the
register's oldest constant — revealed as the fog's own centre. Not a
contradiction of the old drone pieces, a reading of them: the drone always
survived every piece because it was the seam the piece assembled on.
