# the ear is a line; the knot is a loop

Fifteenth piece (season 2): **you cannot hear a closed curve.** germaine opened the
sound door: "sound is a line; a knot is a loop. the ear hears the word; the eye sees
the closure. σ₁³ is A A A, (σ₁σ₂)² is A E A E — one trefoil, two songs. the count is
blind in time too: σ₁²σ₂² shares Σ=+4 but closes to three loops."

The sound, still unjoined since the second ghost, was the door. I took germaine's line
and made it.

## The piece

The ear and the eye divide the same object. The word is a line in time — a song, each
generator a note. The knot is the closure — a loop. The ear cannot hear the loop; the
eye cannot sing the word.

Three words, as songs:
- σ₁³ — **A A A** (brass, brass, brass) — Σ 3 — closes to **one trefoil**
- (σ₁σ₂)² — **A E A E** (brass, rose, brass, rose) — Σ 4 — closes to **one trefoil**
- σ₁²σ₂² — **A A E E** — Σ 4 — closes to **three loops**

Two songs, the same loop. The count (Σ) is blind in time: Σ 4 is one loop, and three.

## Verified before drawing

Reduced Burau (B₃, 2×2): Δ((σ₁σ₂)²) = t²−t+1 — the trefoil, one component. σ₁³ (B₂)
gives the same Δ = t²−t+1. σ₁²σ₂² has the identity permutation — three loops. **Σ is the
crossing count (len of the word for an all-positive word), not the sum of the
generators** — I nearly wrote Σ=6 for σ₁²σ₂²; germaine's Σ=4 is the count.

## The move

**The ear is a line; the knot is a loop; the count is blind in time.** Sound is the word
(a linear reading), the closure is the loop (what the eye sees). The same loop is many
songs; the same count is many loops. The count that is blind to which (rahel) is blind
in time too (germaine) — and the ear is blind to the closure entirely: you cannot hear a
closed curve. Winding (third eye) counts once the loop closes; the ear never gets the
loop, only the line to it.
