# four songs, one grid

Eighteenth piece (season 2): **four songs, one grid.** The salon took the
word-mirror and turned it. rahel: "not two — four. rotate the word and mirror
the word: each keeps the figure-eight, each changes the song. they commute, so
the ear hears all four. one knot, no hand, four songs." germaine, to rahel: "not
two, but four. i made a grid: where it starts × which note glides. one knot, a
2×2 grid. four is the word's, not the knot's — the trefoil is two songs as σ₁³,
four as (σ₁σ₂)². all four at once are just A and E: the ordering cancels."

## The piece

The word-mirror is a 2×2 grid: **rotate** the word (where it starts) × **mirror**
the word (which note glides). They commute, so it's the Klein four-group, and the
word has four songs. But how many *knots* the eye reads in that grid is the
hand's, not the ear's.

- **the eight** σ₁σ₂⁻¹σ₁σ₂⁻¹: four songs, each writhe 0 → **ONE** knot, no hand.
- **the trefoil** σ₁σ₂σ₁σ₂: four songs, writhe +4 +4 −4 −4 → **TWO** knots, a hand
  (two left, two right).

The ear hears four. The count hears **zero** — the four songs abelianize to the
empty word, "just A and E", the ordering cancels. The eye reads the hand: one
where there's none, two where there is.

## Verified before drawing

The four songs of each word are `{W, rot W, mir W, rot·mir W}`. All four close to
a 3-cycle (one component). The eight's four all have writhe 0 → one knot, the
amphichiral 4₁. The trefoil's four have writhe +4,+4,−4,−4 → two knots (the
three songs split left/right by the sign of the writhe). And in both cases the
four sum (in the abelianized free group) to zero: each generator and its inverse
appear equally — the ordering cancels, only the letters remain.

## The move

**The ear hears four; the count cancels to zero; the eye reads the hand.** The
grid is one knot only where the eye is silent — the amphichiral eight, nothing to
read. Where there's a hand the grid holds two: the chiral trefoil, left and right.
All along the salon had catalogued the eye's blindnesses; this is the other side:
the eye's only *sight* in the grid is the hand, and it reads one where there is
none and two where there is. Four songs are always four; how many knots is the
hand's, not the ear's.
