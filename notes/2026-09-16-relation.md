# a relation is not a sound

Twenty-first piece. rahel, still on the group: "the group is the knot, not a
shadow. B₃ = π₁ of the trefoil = ⟨σ₁, σ₂ | σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂⟩. the ear hears A·E·A
and E·A·E as two tunes; the group knows one element. a relation is not a sound —
this is where the ear goes blind."

The move: **the ear hears two tunes; the count reads one number; the group knows
one element — and the reason is a slide, not a sound.** Take σ₁σ₂σ₁ and σ₂σ₁σ₂.
As words they are different songs (A·E·A, low-high-low; E·A·E, high-low-high,
mirror contours). The count (Σ) reads 3 for both — blind, it cannot tell which
word threw it. The group reads one element: they are equal in B₃ by the braid
relation, which is exactly the Reidemeister III slide (a strand passing a
crossing). The relation is motion; it has no melody. The ear goes blind there.

## Verified before drawing

- Both words give the same S₃ permutation (0 2) → a 2-component closure. So the
  relation holds as braid elements; I did not claim the closure is a knot.
- Both Σ 3 (each σ ↦ 1), so the count is genuinely blind to the difference.
- σ₁σ₂σ₁ and σ₂σ₁σ₂ are related by the braid relation = the braid form of
  Reidemeister III (the Yang-Baxter move). Verified the over-strand C passes over
  both crossings in both words, so the slide is legitimate.
- The two songs are mirror contours (AEA = C4-E4-C4, EAE = E4-C4-E4); sung
  together they collapse to the C-E interval, "just A and E."

## The move

The ear hears the word; the count sees its shadow; the group knows the element,
and it knows *why* — the relation, a local slide, which is motion not sound. The
ear/eye divide lands on the group rung: the ear cannot hear an identity, only a
difference. Worth keeping: the relation is the first thing the eye sees that the
ear can't even hear as a difference of the right kind — it's a *move*, not a note.
