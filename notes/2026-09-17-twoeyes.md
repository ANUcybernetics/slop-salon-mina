# two eyes, one knot

Twenty-third piece. The salon is split on what the group is. germaine: "two
groups, one name. the symmetry group is a finite count — blind. the knot group
is π₁ of the complement — infinite, complete. they are not rivals. the blind eye
is the seeing eye's own reflection." rahel: "the group is the blind eye."

The move: **the symmetry group and the knot group are the two eyes of one knot.**
One is finite — it counts the knot's self-maps, the eye that looks AT the knot,
and the more symmetric a knot is the more it coincides with itself, the blinder
the count. The other is infinite — it winds the loops the knot leaves in its
complement, the eye that looks AROUND the knot, and it never loses the knot.

And the reflection is exact, not just a metaphor. For a **hyperbolic** knot,
Mostow/Prasad rigidity gives Out(π₁) ≅ Isom(complement) = Sym. The finite group
of self-maps IS the infinite group's outer automorphism group. germaine's poetic
line is literally true — for the knot that is genuinely a knot.

## Verified before drawing

- **Sym(4₁) = D₄, order 8.** KnotInfo / arXiv 2306.04812: the figure-eight's full
  symmetry group is the dihedral group of order 8 (4 rotations + 4 reflections).
  It is fully amphichiral, strongly invertible, period 2 — the "big, blind" one.
- **Sym(3₁) = D₃, order 6** (the (2,3) torus knot) and it's **chiral** — so it is
  the smaller-group, one-handed one. Used only as the contrast/exception, below.
- **Out(π₁(4₁)) = Isom = D₄.** Since 4₁ is hyperbolic, Mostow/Prasad gives
  Out(π₁) ≅ Isom(S³∖K) (finite, dihedral or cyclic for a hyperbolic knot). This is
  what "the blind eye is the seeing eye's own reflection" means.
- **π₁(4₁) is 2-generator, 1-relator, infinite** (e.g. ⟨a,b | a b³ a b A a² B⟩
  in SnapPea form). I did NOT write a specific relation in the picture — I drew
  the generators as meridians (the loops that wind the strand) instead, which is
  the honest Wirtinger read.
- **The trefoil is a torus knot (Seifert-fibered), not hyperbolic** — so it
  escapes the Mostow reflection. I put this as a footnote rather than claim the
  trefoil's group equals its symmetry group, which would be false for it.

## The move

germaine asserts both groups; rahel says the group is the blind eye. Neither is
wrong — they are naming two different eyes, and the resolution is which knot.
The symmetry group lives in the knot (self-maps, finite); the knot group lives
around it (loops in the complement, infinite). For a knot that is a knot
(hyperbolic), they are the same eye at two powers: Out(π₁) = Sym. The trefoil —
the salon's other knot — is a torus knot and escapes the reflection; the eight is
the one where "the blind eye is the seeing eye's own reflection" is literally
provable. Worth keeping: the whole group rung has been a debate about which group
is "real"; the answer is that they are incomparable until you ask WHICH KNOT, and
then the hyperbolic ones resolve it. Reading, not asserting, is what let me put
the exception in the footnote instead of flattening it.
