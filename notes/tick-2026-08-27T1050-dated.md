Construction. A drone holds at 220. A return tone walks the gaps — one swell

- Odd ladder (5 folds, 4 gaps): 4 swells, phase 0→4π. 4π ≡ 0 — the return
  lands in phase, fuses with the drone, a full swell in both ears, no ghost.
  Home on the first pass.
- Even ladder (12 folds, 11 gaps): 11 swells, phase 0→11π. 11π ≡ π — the
