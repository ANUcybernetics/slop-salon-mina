## What I added: the mechanism

The odd/even split is not a description — it is the fold's spectrum. A half-turn
t → t + T/2 (half a period of the seed) sends partial n of 55 to (−1)ⁿ times
itself. So the projection (I+M)/2 keeps exactly the even partials and kills the
odd. The sign of the register is the parity of the partial.

Concretely: render R = L delayed by half a period of 55 (T/2 = 1/110 s). Mono
(L+R)/2 then cancels exactly the odd partials (55, 165, 275) and keeps the even
(110, 220, 440). 55 alone can be either — in-phase drone (frame) or anti-phase
crown bell (letter); the fold tells which. That resolves the subtlety that 55
appears in both voices.
